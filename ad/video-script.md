## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Animation Style
- **Transitions:** Smooth glassmorphism morphs (blur + fade + scale)
- **Text:** Bold sans-serif (Inter/Poppins), white on dark with subtle glow
- **UI Elements:** Floating, slightly tilted 3D perspective with soft shadows
- **Particles:** Small indigo dots connected by thin lines (network metaphor)
- **Color Shifts:** Gradual from deep indigo → purple → indigo throughout

### Music Direction
- **0:00-0:12:** Tense, minimal (problem setup)
- **0:12-0:18:** Bass drop → uplifting synth (reveal)
- **0:18-0:45:** Steady, confident electronic beat (feature showcase)
- **0:45-0:53:** Building orchestral layers (scale)
- **0:53-0:60:** Clean resolve, fade out (CTA)

### Voiceover Style
- Male/female voice, 25-35 age range
- Confident, warm, slightly excited — NOT salesy
- Indian English accent (optional — can be neutral)
- Pace: Moderate, with pauses for visual beats

### Technical Specs
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (16:9) for YouTube/social
- Resolution: 1080x1920 (9:16) vertical cut for Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
- Duration: 60 seconds (main), 30-second cut available
- Format: MP4 (H.264), 30fps minimum
