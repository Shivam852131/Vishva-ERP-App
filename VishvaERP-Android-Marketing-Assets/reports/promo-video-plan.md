## Editing notes

- Source PNGs are 1080 × 2400 (taller than 9:16). For Reels, place the phone
  screenshot on a branded background (app blue `#020617` / accent `#1D4ED8`)
  rather than cropping — keeps the status bar and full UI visible.
- Animate with slow Ken-Burns zoom (5–8 %) + a highlight circle or arrow on the
  key UI element per shot.
- Music: upbeat, modern tech; keep UI text legible (no overlay on input fields).
- **Exclude:** login validation errors (`07-…`), OTP screen internals, and the
  "Forgot password?" link (non-functional in the app today).
